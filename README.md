## 🎮 How to Use NUEM

### 1. Launching the Application
After installation, find NUEM in your Applications folder or Launchpad. Click the icon to start the app. You'll see a small icon appear in your menu bar at the top of your screen.

### 2. Opening the Control Panel
Click the NUEM icon in the menu bar to open the main control panel. Here you'll find all the customization options for your fold animations.

### 3. Setting Up Your First Fold
Allow NUEM to access your screen recording permissions. This is necessary for the animation to work. You'll see a system prompt asking for permission—click "Allow" to proceed.

### 4. Choosing an Animation Trigger
Decide when you want the fold animation to play. You can choose from:

- **App Launch**: Animate when you open any application
- **Window Switch**: Fold when you switch between windows
- **Sleep Mode**: Animate when your MacBook sleeps or locks
- **Manual Trigger**: Assign a keyboard shortcut to activate the fold anytime

### 5. Adjusting Animation Settings
Use the sliders and menus in the control panel to customize your experience:

- **Perspective Depth**: How much the screen appears to fold
- **Animation Speed**: How fast the fold completes
- **Snap Intensity**: How quickly the final snap happens
- **Sound Volume**: Adjust hinge and snap audio levels

### 6. Enabling Vibration
Turn on the vibration toggle to feel each fold on your trackpad. You can adjust the vibration strength to your preference.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Animation Not Playing
If you don't see animations, check your screen recording permissions. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording, and make sure NUEM is enabled.

### Sounds Not Working
Ensure your Mac's volume is turned up and not muted. Check the NUEM control panel to confirm sound effects are enabled. If issues persist, restart NUEM.

### App Not Responding
Try quitting NUEM from the menu bar icon and relaunching it. If the problem continues, restart your MacBook.

### Battery Drain
If you notice increased battery usage, switch to "Eco" performance mode in NUEM settings. You can also reduce animation frequency.

## 🧠 Tips and Tricks

### Create a Signature Fold
Experiment with different perspective values to find your perfect fold angle. A shallow fold (around 15-20 degrees) looks subtle and elegant, while deeper folds (45+ degrees) are more dramatic.

### Combine with Focus Modes
Set NUEM to activate when you enter Focus modes like "Work" or "Do Not Disturb." This adds a delightful transition each time you settle into focused work.

### Use Manual Triggers
Assign keyboard shortcuts for quick fold animations. This is perfect for adding flair when presenting or showing off your MacBook to friends.

## 🔄 Uninstalling NUEM

To remove NUEM from your Mac:

1. Quit the application from the menu bar
2. Open Finder and go to Applications
3. Drag NUEM to the Trash
4. Empty your Trash to complete the removal

You may also want to remove the screen recording permission by going to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording and removing NUEM from the list.
